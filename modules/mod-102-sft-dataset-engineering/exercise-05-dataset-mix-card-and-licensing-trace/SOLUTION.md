# mod-102 · exercise-05 — Dataset Mix Card and Licensing Trace — Solution

## 1. Solution overview

This is a documentation exercise. The 100k mix never gets assembled; the deliverable is the paperwork that would ship with it: a mix target description, a sources table with real SPDX identifiers, a mix card that justifies every proportion, a red/yellow/green licensing trace, and a data section of a draft model card. The point is that a security reviewer, a partner team, or a downstream user can read ≤5 pages and answer "should I use this model" without opening any dataset.

Two ideas carry the exercise:

- **Redistribution intent is upstream of everything.** The licensing bar for an open-weights release is different from an internal POC. Decide it in part A; everything downstream enforces it.
- **License lives at two levels — dataset and generator.** A dataset labeled permissive but generated by a proprietary teacher inherits the teacher's Terms of Use. The licensing trace must separate these.

The worked example models an internal → open-weights-research release of a mid-size instruction-tuned chat assistant, borrowing the publicly disclosed Tülu 3 [T3] mix structure as a reference baseline and adapting proportions toward a 100k budget.

## 2. Worked implementation

### 2.1 `mix_target.md` (Part A)

```markdown
# Mix Target

- **What is the model for.** General-purpose English chat assistant, mid-size
  base (7–8B), suitable as a reference instruction-tuned checkpoint for
  research and internal product prototypes.
- **Languages.** English only.
- **Redistribution intent.** Open-weights research release on Hugging Face.
  Non-commercial research is in scope. Commercial redistribution is NOT the
  primary target — if a source's terms require it, we drop that source rather
  than relax the intent.
```

### 2.3 `mix_card.md` (Part C) — worked card

```markdown
# SFT Mix Card — openresearch-chat-7b-sft (100k)

## Target model
General-purpose English chat assistant, 7–8B base, open-weights research release.
English only. No tool-use, no vision, no long-context.

## Category proportions (sum = 100%)
| Category | Share | Rows | Rationale |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| General chat | 40% | 40,000 | The assistant's default behavior; underweighting here hurts helpfulness across the board. |
| Reasoning (math + stepwise) | 25% | 25,000 | Highest-leverage single eval axis (GSM8K / MATH); 25% roughly matches Tülu 3's math share. |
| Code | 20% | 20,000 | Secondary eval axis (HumanEval / MBPP); keeps the model usable for engineering tasks without dominating the mix. |
| Safety refusals + non-refusals | 10% | 10,000 | Underweighting trains an over-compliant model; overweighting trains over-refusal. 10% is the operating-point we defend in `licensing_trace.md` and `mod-106`. |
| Persona + format | 5% | 5,000 | Keeps response shape consistent (concise, direct) without crowding out capability data. |

## Per-source table
| Source | License (SPDX) | Category | Filtered size | Target rows | Factor |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| OpenAssistant/oasst1 (en, asst-term) | Apache-2.0 | general_chat | ~41k | 18,000 | 0.44× down |
| HuggingFaceH4/no_robots | CC-BY-NC-4.0 | general_chat | 10k | 8,000 | 0.80× down |
| allenai/wildchat-1m (en, non-prohibited) | ODC-BY-1.0 (+ AI2 ImpACT) | general_chat | ~400k | 12,000 | 0.03× down |
| GAIR/lima | CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 | general_chat | 1k | 2,000 | 2.00× up |
| nvidia/OpenMathInstruct-2 | CC-BY-4.0 | reasoning | ~14M | 15,000 | 0.001× down |
| microsoft/orca-math-word-problems-200k | MIT | reasoning | 200k | 10,000 | 0.05× down |
| bigcode/self-oss-instruct-sc2-exec-filter-50k | ODC-BY-1.0 | code | 50k | 12,000 | 0.24× down |
| ise-uiuc/Magicoder-OSS-Instruct-75K | MIT (+ OpenAI ToU) | code | 75k | 8,000 | 0.11× down |
| ultrafeedback_safety / allenai/tulu-3-sft-mixture | ODC-BY-1.0 | safety | ~40k | 10,000 | 0.25× down |
| synthetic persona-and-format set (exercise-04 style) | Llama-3.1 Comm (teacher) | persona/format | 5k | 5,000 | 1.00× |

**Row counts tie out to 100,000.**

## Per-category rationale
- **General chat (40%)** — Default behavior is what the model is for; any lower and OASST-style diversity gets crowded out by task-specific data.
- **Reasoning (25%)** — Math / stepwise reasoning is the single most eval-visible axis at this size. 25% matches Tülu 3's published ratios closely enough to port their findings. [T3]
- **Code (20%)** — Enough to preserve HumanEval / MBPP performance; higher crowds reasoning-and-chat without proportional gains at 7–8B.
- **Safety (10%)** — Below 10%, benign-refusal rates creep up. See mod-106 for the ablation that pinned this number to 10% for our target behavior.
- **Persona / format (5%)** — Floor needed for shape consistency; higher dilutes capability data.

## Known biases inherited from sources
1. **English STEM-lean.** OASST tilts toward English STEM contributors; MetaMathQA / Orca-Math are Western-notation math. The model will under-serve non-STEM chat and non-English speakers.
2. **GPT-family synthetic footprint.** Half of reasoning and code sources are GPT-3.5/4 distillations; the model inherits those teachers' response shape (bullet-heavy, verbose) and political-leaning priors documented in third-party audits.
3. **Over-represented "helpful" bias.** WildChat and no_robots skew to instruction-following; safety data at 10% may underweight refusal relative to a production chat deployment.
4. **Age bias.** Most sources were curated 2023–2024; events after cutoff are not represented in the mix's explicit factual content.

## What we did not include and why
- **ShareGPT (bilingual / older dumps)** — unclear provenance; user-submitted ChatGPT conversations whose terms are disputed. Redistribution risk > benefit for a research release.
- **Dolly 15k** — license is clean (CC-BY-SA-3.0) but the content overlaps OASST without materially adding diversity at our size.
- **Any dataset labeled `license: other` or `license: unknown`** — hard stop given our redistribution intent.
- **Claude / GPT-4-generated safety datasets** — generator ToU conflicts with open-weights release; we use OASST and Tülu safety splits instead.
```

### 2.4 `licensing_trace.md` (Part D) — worked trace

```markdown
# Licensing Trace — openresearch-chat-7b-sft

Redistribution intent: open-weights research release on Hugging Face (non-commercial research primary; commercial redistribution NOT in scope).

## Verdict table

| Source | SPDX / Terms | Generator terms | Verdict | Obligation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OpenAssistant/oasst1 | Apache-2.0 | n/a | GREEN | Attribution in model card. |
| HuggingFaceH4/no_robots | CC-BY-NC-4.0 | n/a | YELLOW | Non-commercial only. Attribution. |
| GAIR/lima | CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 | n/a | YELLOW | Non-commercial AND share-alike. Downstream must keep compatible license. |
| nvidia/OpenMathInstruct-2 | CC-BY-4.0 | Llama 3.1 Community License (teacher) | YELLOW | Attribution to both dataset and teacher; include "Built with Llama" per community license; carry the MAU clause into the model card. |
| microsoft/orca-math-word-problems-200k | MIT (dataset) | OpenAI Terms of Use (teacher GPT-4) | YELLOW | MIT on data is fine; OpenAI ToU on outputs restricts training competing models. Research redistribution: acceptable per the intent above; commercial downstream: NOT acceptable — flag in model card. <!-- pending-citation: current OpenAI Terms of Use on outputs-for-training, URL + effective date --> |
| meta-math/MetaMathQA | MIT | OpenAI ToU | YELLOW | Same as above. |
| bigcode/self-oss-instruct-sc2-exec-filter-50k | ODC-BY-1.0 | BigCode OpenRAIL-M | YELLOW | Open-RAIL use restrictions carry into downstream; model card states them. |
| ise-uiuc/Magicoder-OSS-Instruct-75K | MIT | OpenAI ToU | YELLOW | Same OpenAI-ToU note. |
| allenai/wildchat-1m | ODC-BY-1.0 + AI2 ImpACT | OpenAI ToU | YELLOW | AI2 ImpACT restrictions on high-risk use carry into the model card; OpenAI ToU as above. |
| synthetic persona-and-format (teacher: Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct) | Llama 3.1 Community License | — | YELLOW | Community license obligations; "Built with Llama" naming; MAU clause. |

- **No RED rows.** Any source that would have been red (ShareGPT, Alpaca dumps if GPT-3's ToU is strict, unknown-license datasets) was dropped in the mix card.
- **One intentional YELLOW cluster** is the OpenAI-ToU-derived reasoning + code sources. Resolution: the research redistribution intent is compatible; the model card explicitly states that commercial redistribution of models trained on this checkpoint must be independently evaluated against OpenAI's then-current ToU. Alternative: replace with OpenMathInstruct-2 and non-GPT code sources only; shrinks reasoning diversity but removes the ToU note. Trade-off recorded here.

## Resolution per YELLOW row

- **CC-BY-NC-4.0 (no_robots)**: model card declares the resulting weights non-commercial for any use case that materially relies on this data. Attribution line in the data section.
- **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 (lima)**: downstream derivatives share-alike. Model card's own license text must be compatible; we adopt a non-commercial research license to discharge this. If a downstream user wants commercial use, lima-sourced rows would need to be excluded and the model re-trained.
- **OpenAI-ToU rows**: model card's "Synthetic data disclosure" section names the teacher per row category, attaches the ToU reference, and advises that commercial downstream users must evaluate ToU currency.
- **Llama Community License rows**: model name carries "Llama"-prefixed naming ("openresearch-chat-7b-llama-sft"), model card includes the MAU clause and "Built with Llama" attribution.
- **BigCode OpenRAIL-M**: use-restriction clauses inherited into the model card (no use for targeted surveillance, etc.). Verbatim restriction list in the data section.
- **AI2 ImpACT (WildChat)**: inherited use restrictions on high-risk deployments; stated in model card limitations.

## Model-card license conclusion

Given the mix, the resulting model is distributed under a **custom research-only
license** that composes the following constraints:
- Non-commercial research use (from CC-BY-NC-4.0 and CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 sources).
- Share-alike for derivatives that fine-tune on this checkpoint and incorporate LIMA-derived data (from CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0).
- "Built with Llama" naming + MAU clause (from Llama 3.1 Community License).
- OpenRAIL-M use restrictions (from BigCode source).
- Synthetic-data attribution to the teacher model(s) used.

The simplest shippable path: **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 for the model weights**, with an addendum file listing Llama / OpenRAIL / OpenAI-ToU-derived obligations.
```

### 2.5 `model_card_data_section.md` (Part E) — worked draft

```markdown
# Model Card — Data Section (openresearch-chat-7b-sft)

## Sources
The SFT mix is 100,000 examples assembled from ten sources across five
capability categories. See `mix_card.md` for per-source counts and
`licensing_trace.md` for the per-source license and generator trace.

## Data collection and preprocessing
- Rows are the output of the pipeline in mod-102 exercise-01 (structural,
  language, length, PII, toxicity, response-quality, dedup) applied per source.
- Eval decontamination (mod-102 exercise-02) run against MMLU, HumanEval,
  GSM8K, MT-Bench at n=13 strict; MT-Bench and GSM8K also under an embedding
  backstop at cosine ≥ 0.9. 61 rows removed by the union of eval passes.
- Multi-turn conversations rendered with each source's native chat template;
  training labels mask non-assistant tokens (mod-102 exercise-03).

## Synthetic data disclosure
Four of the ten sources are entirely or partially synthetic:
- `nvidia/OpenMathInstruct-2` — teacher Llama 3.1 405B Instruct (Llama Community License).
- `microsoft/orca-math-word-problems-200k` — teacher GPT-4 (OpenAI ToU).
- `meta-math/MetaMathQA` — teacher GPT-3.5 / GPT-4 (OpenAI ToU).
- `ise-uiuc/Magicoder-OSS-Instruct-75K` — teacher GPT-3.5 (OpenAI ToU).
- `bigcode/self-oss-instruct-sc2-exec-filter-50k` — teacher StarCoder2 15B (OpenRAIL-M).
- Internal persona/format set (5k rows) — teacher Llama 3.1 70B Instruct (Llama Community License).

Commercial downstream users must independently evaluate the teachers' then-current terms of use.

## Data mix and rationale
See `mix_card.md`. Category shares: general chat 40%, reasoning 25%, code 20%,
safety 10%, persona/format 5%. Proportions were chosen to approximate Tülu 3's
public mix [T3] with a lower code share and no long-context data, matching our
7–8B chat-assistant target.

## Personal data and safety data
- PII: dropped at the filter stage (no redaction retention). Presidio hits on
  EMAIL_ADDRESS / PHONE_NUMBER / CREDIT_CARD / US_SSN / IBAN_CODE / IP_ADDRESS
  removed rows outright. No per-user identifiers survive to the mix.
- Safety data: 10% of the mix is explicit safety training (refusals of
  harmful requests paired with appropriate non-refusals of benign requests).
  See mod-106 for the operating-point ablation.

## Reproducibility
- Dataset snapshot dates: all sources pinned to commit SHAs on 2026-11-10.
- Filter and decontamination configurations live in `configs/`.
- The exact assembly script would be deterministic; this exercise does not
  materialize the 100k file.

## Known limitations
- English-only coverage.
- STEM bias in reasoning; Western-notation math.
- Assistant style inherits GPT-family verbosity patterns from the synthetic sources.
- Safety floor at 10% does not match production-chat operating points; expect
  benign-refusal regressions in high-stakes deployments.
- Eval contamination addressed only for the four big-four evals; other evals
  must be run through the mod-102 exercise-02 pipeline before trusting results.
```

### 2.6 Design rationale (decision table)

| Choice | Why | Alternative | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open-weights research redistribution intent | Makes the licensing bar the only constraint that forces decisions | Internal-only | Internal-only hides license conflicts; they re-emerge at any external release |
| Steal Tülu 3 ratios as baseline | Published, defensible, close enough to port learnings | Design proportions from scratch | Starting from scratch → no eval numbers to justify the ratios against |
| Drop ShareGPT rather than argue the license | Provenance disputes alone are enough to stop auto-merge | Keep and footnote | A reviewer rejects the release the first time they hit the footnote |
| Separate dataset license and generator license | Vendor ToU moves independently of the dataset's SPDX | Collapse into one column | Hides the ToU-conflict risks on MIT-labeled GPT-sourced data |
| Red/yellow/green verdict | Forces a decision per source | Prose discussion | Prose hides the boundary case; the verdict table forces a yes/no |
| Non-commercial-research model license conclusion | Composes the strictest inherited terms | Permissive wrapper | A permissive wrapper violates CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 downstream share-alike |

## 3. Validation steps

1. **Row-count arithmetic.** Per-source target rows in `mix_card.md` sum to 100,000. Per-category proportions sum to 100%. These are mechanical checks; if either misses, the mix card is wrong.
2. **SPDX validity.** Every license column value is either a current SPDX identifier (<https://spdx.org/licenses/>) or an explicit non-SPDX note ("Llama 3.1 Community License", "OpenAI ToU"). Free-form strings like "open-source" or "permissive" are not acceptable.
3. **Verdict sweep.** No `RED` rows in the final mix. Every `YELLOW` row has a matching paragraph in `licensing_trace.md` resolving the obligation.
4. **Model card tie-out.** Every source in `sources.csv` appears in `mix_card.md`'s per-source table, and the data section in `model_card_data_section.md` discloses every synthetic source's teacher.
5. **Reviewer walk.** Hand the five files to a teammate unfamiliar with the project. Ask: "Can I commercially fine-tune this model?" "Did it see MMLU?" "What is its license?" Expect three correct answers in under five minutes.
6. **Dataset-card currency check.** For two randomly selected sources, re-open the HF dataset page and verify the SPDX in the card matches the one in `sources.csv`. HF cards change; if either diverges, update the row (and note the drift in commit history).

## 4. Rubric

Grade on five dimensions, 2 points each, 10 total.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Target + intent** | No `mix_target.md`, or intent not stated | Intent stated but language scope or use-case fuzzy | All three questions answered; redistribution intent is specific enough to drive source decisions |
| **Sources table** | <4 categories, or missing license/generator columns | Correct structure; one or two SPDXes are free-form | ≥4 categories, every row has a real SPDX or a documented non-SPDX note, generator split out when synthetic |
| **Mix card** | Proportions don't sum to 100% or not justified | Sums, but biases/exclusions sections missing | Proportions sum, per-category rationale, biases, "did not include" all present |
| **Licensing trace** | No red/yellow/green table, or red rows left in mix | Table present but obligations not resolved | No red; every yellow has a resolution; model-card license conclusion named |
| **Model card data section** | Missing or unanswerable by a reader | Covers sources but omits synthetic disclosure or PII | Readable in 5 min; answers "should I use this model" |

**Pass threshold:** 7/10, no single dimension at 0.

## 5. Common mistakes

- Choosing "internal only" as the intent to sidestep license work. The conflict reappears the first time anyone proposes an external release.
- Leaving `license: "other"` or `license: "unknown"` in `sources.csv`. If it cannot be an SPDX or a named non-SPDX obligation, drop the source.
- Collapsing dataset license and generator license into one string. An MIT dataset generated with GPT-4 inherits OpenAI's ToU on content.
- Writing a mix card with per-category percentages but no per-source row counts. Downstream reproduction can't build the mix from percentages alone.
- Keeping CC-BY-SA / CC-BY-NC-SA sources and then shipping the model under a permissive license. Share-alike bites downstream.
- Omitting the "what we did not include and why" section. Reviewers ask for it; writing it up front saves a round of comments.
- Claiming decontamination in the data section without pointing at the exercise-02 artifact.
- Treating the HF dataset card's license field as ground truth without reading the repo LICENSE file. They disagree surprisingly often.
